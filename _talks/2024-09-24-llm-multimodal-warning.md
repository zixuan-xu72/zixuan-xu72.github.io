I presented our LLM-based Multimodal Warning system (LLM-MW) in an invited session at IEEE ITSC 2024 in Edmonton, Canada.

The talk introduced how the system interprets the traffic environment and driver profiles to plan and deliver personalized multimodal warnings, and discussed directions for personalized driver assistance.
